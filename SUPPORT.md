# Support

MB X7 Control is a community project and is not affiliated with or supported
by Creative Technology Ltd.

Before reporting a problem:

1. Confirm the Sound Blaster X7 is connected directly by USB and powered on.
2. Confirm macOS can see the X7 as an audio device.
3. Restart MB X7 Control and use its refresh button.
4. Record the macOS version, Mac model, X7 firmware version if known, speaker
   or headphone configuration, and the exact action that failed.

Use the
[`alcatron/mb-x7-control` issue tracker](https://github.com/alcatron/mb-x7-control/issues)
for reproducible bugs and feature requests. Do not post passwords, serial
numbers, personal data, or copyrighted Creative binaries and frameworks.

The project does not promise individual support or response times. Donations
are optional and do not purchase priority support, functionality, or updates.
