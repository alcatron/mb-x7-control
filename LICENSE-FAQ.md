# Licence FAQ

MB X7 Control is free and source-available under the MIT License with the
Commons Clause License Condition v1.0.

This page is a plain-language summary. The terms in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE)
control if this summary and the licence differ.

## What is allowed?

- Personal use is allowed.
- Internal use by a business or other organisation is allowed.
- You may inspect, study and modify the source code.
- You may contribute changes to the project.
- You may share source code, modifications and compiled builds without charge,
  provided you preserve the required copyright and licence notices and follow
  the separate branding terms in [`TRADEMARKS.md`](TRADEMARKS.md).

## What is not allowed?

The licence does not grant permission to sell MB X7 Control. This includes
charging for the application, paid redistribution, or offering a product or
service whose value derives entirely or substantially from MB X7 Control.

Commercial permission outside the public licence requires prior written
permission from the relevant copyright holders.

## Are donations required?

No. Donations to the official project maintainer are entirely optional. They
do not unlock features, updates or support, and MB X7 Control remains free to
build and use.

## Is this OSI open-source software?

No. Because the Commons Clause restricts selling the software, MB X7 Control
is described as source-available rather than OSI open source.

## Does the software licence include the branding?

No. See [`TRADEMARKS.md`](TRADEMARKS.md) for the separate terms covering the
MB X7 Control name, logo, icon and artwork. Third-party distributed builds must
use distinct branding and must not imply that they are official releases.
